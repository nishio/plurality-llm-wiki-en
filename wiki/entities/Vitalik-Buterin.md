## Background

Vitalik Buterin (b. 1994) is the founder of Ethereum and one of the most prolific public writers in the web3 space. He blogs at `vitalik.eth.limo` (the eth.limo gateway is the current canonical URL; older posts live at `vitalik.ca`). Source repo: `vbuterin/blog` on GitHub.

## Role in the Plurality lineage

- **Co-author** with [[Glen-Weyl]] and Puja Ohlhaver of *Decentralized Society: Finding Web3's Soul* (DeSoc / SBT paper, May 2022), the founding technical paper of soulbound tokens — a key Plurality-adjacent identity primitive.
- **Contributor / endorser** of the *Plurality* book ("In a technologically advanced, politically challenging, and rapidly evolving 21st century, what does a free and open future look like? Glen, Audrey and their coauthors offer a compelling view of a way forward" — quoted on plurality.net).
- **Frequent essayist on Plurality-adjacent themes**: see source pages for the 2022 Soulbound essay, 2023 Three Transitions essay, 2023 *My techno-optimism* / d/acc, 2024 crypto+AI essay, and 2025 *Let a thousand societies bloom*.
- **Video contributor to Civic AI Conference 2026** (Oxford): see [[civic-ai-with-us-vitalik]] — "AI With Us, Not Without Us." Tang and Tenzin Yangtso cite this in [[civic-ai-gentle-bridge]] as confirmation that "the garden stops being metaphor."

## Trajectory through the wiki

The essays trace a movement from primarily *cryptographic* framings of Plurality (Soulbound, DeSoc) toward primarily *social-tech* framings:

- **2022 (Soulbound, DeSoc)**: identity and association as cryptographic primitives — SBTs, proof-of-humanity, ZK selective disclosure.
- **2023 (Three Transitions, techno-optimism)**: identity is *not* a single address; the wallet must secure data, not just keys. d/acc as differential optimism. Plurality named as part of the political-defense pillar.
- **2024 (cryptoai)**: AI participates as a *player* in micro-prediction-markets — convergence with the book's mechanism-design layer but via prediction markets rather than bridging algorithms.
- **2025 (societies)**: explicit anti-country-takeover position; tribes (cultural) and zones (jurisdictional) as the realistic vehicles for pluralist experimentation. Web3 vocabulary recedes; physical hubs and immersion become central.
- **2026 (civic-ai-with-us-vitalik, civic.ai video)**: "AI as a bicycle for the mind" — endorses Tang's bounded-Kami framing in the [[Civic-AI]] 6-Pack of Care framework, advocates for one-model-per-relationship customisation, warns against AI monoculture.

## Divergences from the book / Tang

- **AI governance**: Buterin prefers *micro-prediction-markets with AI players* ([[vitalik-cryptoai]]); Tang prefers *bridging deliberation with humans in the loop* ([[Civic-AI]]). Both reject centralised arbiters of truth but propose different substitutes.
- **Scale of experimentation**: the book and Tang focus on *plural publics inside democracies*; Buterin's *societies* essay focuses on *physical zones with explicit legal autonomy* (Prospera, Gelephu Mindfulness City). Adjacent but not coextensive.
- **Network-state distancing**: Buterin reviewed [[Network-State]] sympathetically in 2022 but had explicitly distanced himself by 2025, naming the "country takeover" turn of the Tech Right as "deeply scary."
