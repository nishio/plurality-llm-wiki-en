## Role

From [[plurality-book]], Audrey Tang is:

- co-author of the book with [[Glen-Weyl]];
- **inaugural Minister of Digital Affairs of Taiwan** (2022–), having earlier served as Digital Minister from 2016;
- **inaugural "digital" minister** of the Plurality Institute community.

She is identified in the book's three-part definition of [[Plurality]] with the **prescriptive** component — the agenda of building the technology engines that harness diversity rather than letting it combust.

## Biographical arc in the book

The book recounts her arrival in public service via [[Sunflower-Movement]]-era "reverse mentor" recruitment by Jaclyn Tsai. She was one of the world's first ministers in charge of digital participation. Her role expanded as the Ministry of Digital Affairs (數位部) was established in 2022.

## Why 數位

Her ministerial title in Mandarin, **數位**, simultaneously means "digital" (applied to technology) and "plural" (applied to people). [[plurality-book]] uses this conflation as a central conceptual move — the title itself embodies Plurality.

## Beyond the book

- Long-standing open-source contributor (Haskell, Perl 6).
- Identified with anarchist / conservative-anarchist tendencies in her own self-description.
- Key figure in the [[g0v]] civic-tech community and the [[vTaiwan]] platform.
