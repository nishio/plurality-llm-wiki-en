## Role

From [[plurality-book]], Audrey Tang is:

- co-author of the book with [[Glen-Weyl]];
- **inaugural Minister of Digital Affairs of Taiwan** (2022–), having earlier served as Digital Minister from 2016;
- **inaugural "digital" minister** of the Plurality Institute community.

She is identified in the book's three-part definition of [[Plurality]] with the **prescriptive** component — the agenda of building the technology engines that harness diversity rather than letting it combust.

## Biographical arc in the book

The book recounts her arrival in public service via [[Sunflower-Movement]]-era "reverse mentor" recruitment by Jaclyn Tsai. She was one of the world's first ministers in charge of digital participation. Her role expanded as the Ministry of Digital Affairs (數位部) was established in 2022.

## Why 數位

Her ministerial title in Mandarin, **數位**, simultaneously means "digital" (applied to technology) and "plural" (applied to people). [[plurality-book]] uses this conflation as a central conceptual move — the title itself embodies Plurality.

## Beyond the book

- Long-standing open-source contributor (Haskell, Perl 6).
- Identified with anarchist / conservative-anarchist tendencies in her own self-description.
- Key figure in the [[g0v]] civic-tech community and the [[vTaiwan]] platform.

## Updates

### Post-ministerial trajectory (from [[audreyt-org-profile]])

After her tenure as Minister of Digital Affairs ended in 2024, Tang took on the **Cyber Ambassador** role for Taiwan and became an **inaugural Senior Accelerator Fellow at the Oxford Institute for Ethics in AI**. She also serves as **Guest Curator for TED 2026**, Omidyar Senior Advisor at Mozilla, Senior Fellow at Project Liberty Institute, and Plurality Initiative Advisor to the Ethereum Foundation.

She received the **2025 Right Livelihood Award** (the "alternative Nobel Prize") for "advancing the social use of digital technology to empower citizens, renew democracy and heal divides." Her Stockholm acceptance speech coined the phrase "geothermal democracy": "the crashing plates turned into positive energy for renewal." She was also named a **2025 AI World Society World Leader** by the Boston Global Forum and a **2023 TIME 100 AI**.

### Self-description: techno-communitarianism

From [[audreyt-org-profile]]: Tang now describes her project as **techno-communitarianism** — "a synthesis of the Plurality framework with [[Glen-Weyl]], and Patrick Deneen's communitarian critique of liberalism — as a theory of Civic AI that fosters rather than atomises community."

### Civic AI / 6-Pack of Care (2025–)

From [[civic-ai-manifesto]] (Google DeepMind, Sep 2025) and [[Civic-AI]]: with [[Caroline-Green]] at Oxford, Tang developed the [[Civic-AI]] 6-Pack of Care framework, grounded in [[Joan-Tronto]]'s political ethics of care. Civic AI is positioned as the AI-governance face of [[Plurality]]: a horizontal alternative to the "singularity" trajectory, where AI is the bounded local *Kami* rather than the universal *gardener*.

### Operational philosophy: "complete the work and step back"

From [[audreyt-org-profile]]: Tang frames her ministerial exit as the operational form of Pack 6 ("Symbiosis / Kami of Care"): "the platforms, the methodology, and the legislation outlasted the tenure, which is what 'complete the work and step back' was designed to mean in practice."

### Concrete legislative win: Taiwan anti-deepfake-scam regime

From [[collaborative-immune-system]] (LDP HQ Tokyo, Dec 2025) and [[ai-alignment-cannot-be-top-down]] (AI Frontiers, Nov 2025): Tang's 2024 Alignment Assembly (200,000 SMS → 447 demographically-representative citizens → 85%+ consensus) produced the *Fraud Crime Hazard Prevention Act* chapter 3 (Articles 27–42) covering KYA verification, transparency labelling, joint platform liability, and graduated enforcement (fines → traffic throttling → DNS blocking). Reported outcomes by November 2025: impersonation scam ads ↓ 95%+, investment scam ads ↓ ~90%, total fraud cases ↓ 25%, financial losses ↓ 50%+.

### Senate of Canada testimony

From [[good-enough-ancestor-senate-canada]] (April 27, 2026): Tang testifies that AI redistributes attention, authority, and bargaining power; she proposes the **right to learn, right to know, right to contest** as the AI-era extension of the right to work. Key phrases introduced or solidified: "data as soil, not oil"; "campfires, not wildfires"; "sovereignty is not solitude."

### Speculative 2040 retrospective

From [[transparent-horse]] (2025): Tang's speculative essay frames the AGI question not as opaque autonomy ("Trojan horse") but as transparent, inspectable, overridable systems ("Transparent horse"). The "decisive variable" for the success of restraint was **legitimacy**, not benchmarks.

### Selected ROOST / Civic AI roles

From [[collaborative-immune-system]]: board member of **ROOST** (Robust Open Online Safety Tools), which provides the `gpt-oss-safeguard` model used in federated safety arrangements with OpenAI, Discord, Bluesky.
