## Description

g0v (pronounced "gov-zero") is the open-source civic-tech community in Taiwan that builds open alternatives and complements to government services. From [[plurality-book]], g0v is a key institutional precondition for Taiwan's [[Digital-Democracy]]: it predates and feeds into [[vTaiwan]], the COVID mask-distribution map, and much of the technology infrastructure that the Ministry of Digital Affairs now formally supports.

## Role in the Plural ecosystem

The book treats g0v as a leading example of the **civil-society-driven** technology development that the [[Policy]] chapter recommends as a global model. It is also cited (alongside [[RadicalxChange]]) as a transnational civic community whose influence extends beyond formal democratic states.

## Relation to government

The g0v community embodies the book's recommendation that public-interest technology be developed in **coopetition** between civil society and government, in the spirit of [[JCR-Licklider]]'s ARPA-funded research network. [[Audrey-Tang]] is among many g0v participants who have moved into and out of government roles.
