## Description

Join (公共政策網路參與平臺) is Taiwan's official government petition and policy-consultation platform, mentioned alongside [[vTaiwan]] in [[plurality-book]] chapter **2-2 The Life of a Digital Democracy** as part of Taiwan's distinctive civic-tech infrastructure.

## Role

Where [[vTaiwan]] grew from the civil-society [[g0v]] community and is consensus-oriented, Join is the formal government channel: citizens submit petitions, and once a petition crosses a participation threshold, the relevant ministry must respond and may convene further deliberation. The two platforms together cover both the civil-society and the state side of Taiwan's [[Digital-Democracy]].
