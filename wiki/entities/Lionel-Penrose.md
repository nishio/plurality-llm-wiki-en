## In the book

[[plurality-book]] cites Lionel Penrose only briefly in chapter **5-6 Plural Voting**: he is credited with the **1946 "square-root rule"** for weighting voting power, which the book identifies as the statistical intuition behind both [[Quadratic-Voting]] and the EU Council's degressive-proportional voting weights. The book pairs his contribution with [[Charles-Dodgson]]'s 1884 work on multi-vote weighting as the intellectual lineage of modern Plural Voting.

## Background

Lionel Sharples Penrose (1898–1972) was a British psychiatrist, medical geneticist, mathematician and chess theorist. He held the Galton Chair of Eugenics (later renamed Human Genetics) at University College London. His 1946 paper *The Elementary Statistics of Majority Voting* proposed that voting power should be proportional to the square root of the size of the represented population — the "Penrose method" used in academic and EU debates over voting weights.
