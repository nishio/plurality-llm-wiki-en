## In the book

[[plurality-book]] mentions Iain M. Banks only briefly in chapter **2-0**, alongside [[Isaac-Asimov]], [[Ray-Kurzweil]], and [[Nick-Bostrom]] as a fictional source of the [[Technocracy]] / superintelligence worldview. His 1987 novel *Consider Phlebas* — the first of the Culture novels — is cited in a footnote. The book also lists the Culture series in a different footnote as one of many sci-fi visions illustrating that technology could coevolve with society in many directions.

## Background

Iain Menzies Banks (1954–2013) was a Scottish novelist who wrote literary fiction as "Iain Banks" and science fiction as "Iain M. Banks". His **Culture series** (1987–2012, ten novels and a story collection) depicts a post-scarcity, semi-anarchist civilization run jointly by humans and benevolent superintelligent AIs ("Minds"), widely cited as one of the most influential treatments of post-scarcity utopia in science fiction.
