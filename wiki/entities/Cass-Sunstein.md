## In the book

[[plurality-book]] cites Cass Sunstein only briefly: in chapter **5-4 [[Augmented-Deliberation]]**, his book *#Republic: Divided Democracy in the Age of Social Media* (2017) is invoked to motivate the use of **bridging algorithms** — recommender systems that surface content earning approval across opinion clusters, rather than maximizing engagement (which tends to entrench filter bubbles per Sunstein's diagnosis). *#Republic* is also footnoted in chapter **2-0** among the canon of polarization-focused books.

## Background

Cass R. Sunstein (b. 1954) is an American legal scholar, the Robert Walmsley University Professor at Harvard Law School. He served as Administrator of the White House Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs (OIRA) from 2009 to 2012. He has written extensively on administrative law, behavioral economics, and digital democracy. His relevant works include *Republic.com* (2001), *Nudge* (with Richard Thaler, 2008), and *#Republic* (2017).
