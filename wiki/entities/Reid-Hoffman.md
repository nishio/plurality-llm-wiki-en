## In the book

[[plurality-book]] names Reid Hoffman only in passing in chapter **2-0**, identifying him as **[[Sam-Altman]]'s mentor** and, with Altman and [[Elon-Musk]], a leading exponent of the [[AGI]]-centered [[Technocracy]] worldview in Silicon Valley.

## Background

Reid Hoffman (b. 1967) is an American entrepreneur, venture capitalist and author. He co-founded LinkedIn and is a partner at Greylock Partners. He was an early board member of OpenAI and has written and spoken publicly on AI and the future of work — among other things co-authoring *Impromptu* (2023), a book written in dialogue with GPT-4.
