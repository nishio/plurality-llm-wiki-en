## In the book

[[plurality-book]] mentions James A. Robinson only in passing, as co-author with [[Daron-Acemoglu]] of *The Narrow Corridor: States, Societies, and the Fate of Liberty* (2020). The "narrow corridor" between social collapse and authoritarianism that the book invokes throughout chapter **2-0** comes from this work.

## Background

James A. Robinson (b. 1960) is a British political scientist and economist, the Reverend Dr. Richard L. Pearson Professor of Global Conflict Studies and University Professor at the University of Chicago Harris School of Public Policy. With [[Daron-Acemoglu]] he co-authored *Why Nations Fail* (2012) and *The Narrow Corridor* (2019). He shared the 2024 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences.
