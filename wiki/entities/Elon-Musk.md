## In the book

[[plurality-book]] mentions Elon Musk only briefly in chapter **2-0**:

- as an **OpenAI co-founder** who, with [[Sam-Altman]], "report having initially sought government funding and only having turned to private, profit-driven sources after being repeatedly turned down";
- and, alongside Altman and Altman's mentor [[Reid-Hoffman]], as a leading Silicon Valley exponent of the [[AGI]]-centered [[Technocracy]] view.

He is referenced as a representative of the worldview the book positions [[Plurality]] against, not engaged with as an individual thinker.

## Background

Elon Musk (b. 1971) is the CEO of Tesla and SpaceX, owner of X (formerly Twitter), and founder of Neuralink and the Boring Company. He co-founded OpenAI in 2015 and left its board in 2018. He is one of the most publicly visible figures in the contemporary technology industry.
