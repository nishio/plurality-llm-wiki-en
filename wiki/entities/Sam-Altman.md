## In the book

[[plurality-book]] mentions Sam Altman briefly in chapter **2-0** "Information Technology and Democracy: a Widening Gulf":

- as an **OpenAI founder** who "report having initially sought government funding and only having turned to private, profit-driven sources after being repeatedly turned down" (with [[Elon-Musk]]);
- as the author of the 2021 essay **"Moore's Law for Everything"**, cited as the canonical articulation of the Technocratic view that AI will make material goods cheap and abundant;
- and as a "leading exponent" of the [[AGI]]-focused Technocratic worldview "in Silicon Valley", along with his mentor [[Reid-Hoffman]] and his former OpenAI co-founder [[Elon-Musk]].

The book uses him as a representative figure of [[Technocracy]] rather than engaging with his work in detail.

## Background

Sam Altman (b. 1985) is an American entrepreneur and investor. He was president of Y Combinator from 2014 to 2019 and is the co-founder and CEO of OpenAI, the AI research lab behind the GPT family of [[Generative-Foundation-Models]]. He has been a public advocate of universal basic income ([[Universal-Basic-Income]]) as a response to AI-driven displacement of labor.
