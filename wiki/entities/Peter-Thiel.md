## In the book

[[plurality-book]] names Peter Thiel only in passing in chapter **2-0**'s survey of "Ideologies of the twenty-first century". He is identified as **"the central intellectual leader"** of the Libertarian / cryptocurrency / Network State worldview, "along with others (such as the authors mentioned) whom he has funded or promoted the work of". The book notes Thiel's emergence as a primary financial supporter of Donald Trump and his connection to British MP Jacob Rees-Mogg, former Austrian Chancellor Sebastian Kurz, and Thiel protégés Blake Masters and J. D. Vance.

The mention is a sketch of one of two **"monist atomism"** worldviews ([[Libertarianism]] and [[Technocracy]]) that [[Plurality]] is offered as an alternative to — not an extended engagement with Thiel's own writing.

## Background

Peter Thiel (b. 1967) is a German-American venture capitalist. He co-founded PayPal and Palantir Technologies, was the first outside investor in Facebook, and co-founded the venture firm Founders Fund. He is the author of *Zero to One* (2014) and is widely associated with techno-libertarian and contrarian political ideas, including funding [[Curtis-Yarvin]] and [[Balaji-Srinivasan]] in adjacent intellectual movements.
