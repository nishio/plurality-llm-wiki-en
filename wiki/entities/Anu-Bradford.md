## In the book

[[plurality-book]] cites Anu Bradford only briefly but in a structurally important place: chapter **7-0 [[Policy]]** opens by adopting her framework from *Digital Empires* (2023), which identifies three competing global digital-policy models:

- the **US** model — neoliberal, private-sector-driven;
- the **PRC** model — state-driven, party-aligned;
- the **EU** model — regulatory powerhouse defending fundamental rights.

The book takes her three-model frame as a foil and positions Taiwan's [[Digital-Democracy]] as a **fourth, Plural model** — civil-society-led, public investment for proactive Plural protocol-building.

## Background

Anu Bradford is the Henry L. Moses Professor of Law and International Organization at Columbia Law School. She is best known for her 2020 book *The Brussels Effect: How the European Union Rules the World*, which argued the EU's regulatory choices propagate globally through market mechanisms. *Digital Empires: The Global Battle to Regulate Technology* (Oxford, 2023) extended the analysis to the US-PRC-EU contest over digital policy.
