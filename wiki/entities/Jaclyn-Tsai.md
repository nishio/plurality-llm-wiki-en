## In the book

[[plurality-book]] mentions Jaclyn Tsai (蔡玉玲) only briefly in chapter **2-1 A View From Yushan**, in the [[Sunflower-Movement]] aftermath:

> "One particularly proactive such minister, one of the world's first ministers in charge of digital participation, Jaclyn Tsai, recruited one of us to begin our journey of public service. Eventually this led to her taking that role in 2016 and in 2022 becoming the first Minister of Digital Affairs."

Tsai is the unsung pivot through which [[Audrey-Tang]] entered the ROC government — a single-paragraph mention that nonetheless shapes the whole [[Digital-Democracy]] story.

## Background

Jaclyn Tsai (蔡玉玲) is a Taiwanese lawyer and government official. She served as Minister Without Portfolio for Taiwan from 2013 to 2016, with a digital and legal-reform portfolio. After the Sunflower Movement, she launched the [[vTaiwan]] consensus-finding platform with civic technologists and brought in "reverse mentors" from civil society — including Audrey Tang — to advise on digital and legal modernization.
