## In the book

[[plurality-book]] names Curtis Yarvin only briefly in chapter **2-0**, listing his writings — published under the pen name **"Mencius Moldbug"** at *Unqualified Reservations* — among the canonical texts of the contemporary [[Libertarianism]] / Network State worldview. He is grouped with James Dale Davidson and Lord William Rees-Mogg (*The Sovereign Individual*), [[Balaji-Srinivasan]] (*The Network State*), and "Bronze Age Pervert" as authors "widely read and cited in the community", with [[Peter-Thiel]] as the central financial / intellectual hub.

The book also flags an "**accelerationist** attitude as advocated by Yarvin, Davidson and Rees-Mogg that views the 'nationalist backlash' to the inevitable technological trends as an accelerant and possible ally in the dissolution of the nation-state."

## Background

Curtis Yarvin (b. 1973) is an American software engineer (creator of the Urbit decentralized computing platform) and political blogger who from 2007 to 2014 wrote *Unqualified Reservations* under the pen name "Mencius Moldbug". His writings are foundational to the "Dark Enlightenment" / neo-reactionary movement, which rejects representative democracy in favor of corporate-style governance.
