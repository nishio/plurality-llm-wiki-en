## Role in the manuscript

Takahiro Anno is the lead worked example added to the **"Frontiers of augmented deliberation"** section of chapter 5-4 [[Augmented-Deliberation]] in the post-PDF upstream ([[pluralitybook-upstream-2024-2026]] PR #1043, merged 2024-12-21). He is also named, by reference, in the PR #1055 sub-section of [[The-Lost-Dao|3-3]] as a "contemporary inheritor" of the [[Nameraka-Society|Nameraka]] / [[Ken-Suzuki|Suzuki]] vision.

## The 2024 Tokyo gubernatorial campaign

From the manuscript (PR #1043 patch):

- **Manifesto on Google Slides and GitHub** — the campaign published its policy platform as a Google Slides deck mirrored to GitHub for issue-based collaborative editing (`takahiroanno2024/election2024`).
- **Multi-channel input collection** — voter feedback came in via X (formerly Twitter), Google Forms, GitHub issues, and an AI-handled phone line.
- **Talk to the City for opinion mapping** — published reports at `takahiroanno2024.github.io/tokyoai-analysis/` visualised the perspectives of the input stream using the AI Objectives Institute's TttC. The book uses this to literalise its own earlier abstract claim about LLM-mediated bridging.
- **GitHub discussion as policy refinement** — over a 15-day window (June 21 – July 6, 2024), **232 issues raised, 104 proposals submitted, 85 adopted**.
- **AI-Anno voice avatar on YouTube** — over a 16-day window the AI avatar answered **~7,400 questions**, ~**77%** of submitted inquiries, "significantly surpassing the capacity of a single human respondent in a lecture format."

Result: **~150,000 votes, 2.3%, 5th place** — "the highest number of votes in history for a candidate in their 30s in the 22 past Tokyo gubernatorial elections."

## Aftermath (also in PR #1043)

- October 15, 2024 — Talk to the City used in a House of Representatives election special program to confront incumbent politicians with citizens' concerns.
- November 22, 2024 onward — Talk to the City used to gather public input for Tokyo's 2050 long-term plan; **>10,000 opinions** collected by December 13 (per the manuscript footnote).
- Per [[civic-ai-manifesto]] (Tang, 2026): Anno's **Team Mirai** has since become a national party in Japan's Diet.

## Why this matters for the wiki森

This is the EN manuscript's **first electoral-politics worked example of augmented deliberation** — the section was previously theoretical ("Soon, it should be possible to go further..."). It also closes a JA → EN loop: the campaign was first widely discussed in Japanese (and likely in the JA wiki); a Japanese contributor (nishio) submitted it as PR #1043 derived from Issue #1007; it now sits in the English root manuscript alongside Polis and Community Notes.
