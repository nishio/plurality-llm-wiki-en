## In the book

[[plurality-book]] mentions Isaac Asimov only briefly in chapter **2-0**, listing him among the science-fiction sources of the [[Technocracy]] worldview: superintelligences in the fiction of "Isaac Asimov and Iain M. Banks, the predictive futurism of [[Ray-Kurzweil]] and [[Nick-Bostrom]], and films like *Terminator* and *Her*." His *I, Robot* (1950) is cited in a footnote. The book uses his fiction as cultural background rather than engaging with it analytically.

## Background

Isaac Asimov (1920–1992) was a Russian-born American writer and biochemistry professor, one of the "Big Three" of twentieth-century science fiction (with Robert A. Heinlein and Arthur C. Clarke). He wrote or edited more than 500 books, including the *Foundation* and *Robot* series. His Three Laws of Robotics, introduced in his 1942 story *Runaround*, remain a touchstone for popular discussions of AI ethics.
