## In the book

[[plurality-book]] mentions Nick Bostrom only briefly in chapter **2-0**:

- alongside [[Ray-Kurzweil]], [[Isaac-Asimov]] and Iain M. Banks as one of the "predictive futurism" / science-fiction exponents of the [[Technocracy]] / [[AGI]] worldview;
- as the author of *Superintelligence* (2014), cited in a footnote;
- as the author of *Deep Utopia: Life and Meaning in a Solved World* (2024), which the book calls **"perhaps the purest expression"** of the Technocratic view.

The mention is in service of characterizing the worldview Plurality positions itself against, not engaging with Bostrom's specific arguments.

## Background

Nick Bostrom (b. 1973) is a Swedish philosopher who founded and led the Future of Humanity Institute at Oxford until its closure in 2024. His work centers on existential risk, the simulation argument, and the long-term ethics of AI. His book *Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies* (2014) was widely influential in the AI safety community.
