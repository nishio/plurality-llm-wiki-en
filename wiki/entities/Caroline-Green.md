## Background

Dr. Caroline Emmer De Albuquerque Green is a researcher at the University of Oxford's Institute for Ethics in AI, specialising in long-term care AI. She co-developed the **6-Pack of Care** governance framework with [[Audrey-Tang]] during Tang's Senior Accelerator Fellowship at Oxford.

## In the wiki

From [[civic-ai-manifesto]] and [[Civic-AI]]: Green and Tang translated [[Joan-Tronto]]'s ethics of care into six interlocking design principles for AI governance, with Tang focusing on the political/civic application and Green grounding the work in long-term-care domain expertise.

From [[ai-alignment-cannot-be-top-down]]: the 6-Pack of Care is the institutional translation Green and Tang developed for the "basic asymmetry: AI operates at speeds and scales beyond human oversight. To keep it aligned, our institutions must evolve to match that tempo by learning, responding, and recalibrating continuously, with people in the loop at every level."
