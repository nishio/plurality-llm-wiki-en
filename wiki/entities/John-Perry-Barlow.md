## In the book

[[plurality-book]] names John Perry Barlow only briefly in chapter **2-0**, when listing peak moments of [[Libertarianism]]: "Libertarianism was arguably at its peak in the 1990s, with John Perry Barlow's 'Declaration of the Independence of Cyberspace', Stephenson's novels and the publication of *The Sovereign Individual*."

The book uses Barlow's "Declaration" as a touchstone for the libertarian conception of the internet that Plurality positions itself against, not as an engagement with his broader work.

## Background

John Perry Barlow (1947–2018) was an American poet, essayist, cattle rancher and Grateful Dead lyricist. He co-founded the Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF) in 1990 and wrote *A Declaration of the Independence of Cyberspace* in 1996 in response to the US Telecommunications Act, declaring that cyberspace was beyond the reach of national governments. The Declaration became a foundational text for cyber-libertarianism.
