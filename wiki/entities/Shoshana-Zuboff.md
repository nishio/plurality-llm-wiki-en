## In the book

[[plurality-book]] cites Shoshana Zuboff only briefly, but in a prominent position: a sentence from her *The Age of Surveillance Capitalism* (2019) is the **first epigraph** of chapter **2-0**:

> "Surveillance capitalism is...a coup from above...an overthrow of the people's sovereignty and a prominent force in the perilous drift towards democratic deconsolidation..."

She is paired with [[Marc-Andreessen]] (whose techno-optimist manifesto is the second epigraph) to frame the polarized debate the book seeks to move beyond. Her diagnosis of [[Surveillance-Capitalism]] is invoked again later in the chapter as part of the case for the "centralizing" threat to democracy.

## Background

Shoshana Zuboff (b. 1951) is an American social psychologist and Charles Edward Wilson Professor Emerita at Harvard Business School. Her *In the Age of the Smart Machine* (1988) and *The Age of Surveillance Capitalism* (2019) are widely cited critiques of how digital technology has reshaped work and reshaped the political economy.
