## In the book

[[plurality-book]] mentions Marc Andreessen only briefly: he supplies the **second epigraph** of chapter **2-0** "Information Technology and Democracy: a Widening Gulf", in the form of a quote from his 2023 essay **"The Techno-Optimist Manifesto"** on the *Andreessen Horowitz Blog*:

> "We are being lied to...told that technology takes our jobs, reduces our wages, increases inequality, threatens our health, ruins the environment, degrades our society..."

The book pairs Andreessen's manifesto with [[Shoshana-Zuboff]]'s *The Age of Surveillance Capitalism* as opposite poles of the contemporary debate over technology that Plurality seeks to transcend.

## Background

Marc Andreessen (b. 1971) is an American entrepreneur and venture capitalist. He co-created the Mosaic web browser, co-founded Netscape, and in 2009 co-founded Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), one of Silicon Valley's largest venture firms. His 2023 "Techno-Optimist Manifesto" became a widely cited statement of pro-technology, growth-focused ideology.
