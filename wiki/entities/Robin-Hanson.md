## In the book

[[plurality-book]] credits Robin Hanson briefly in chapter **5-6 Plural Voting** as the originator of **Futarchy / predictive voting**: "Robin Hanson's proposal: vote on values, bet on beliefs in prediction markets." The book itself uses a hybrid Futarchy-like mechanism for change approval in its production process (contributors simultaneously vote and predict outcomes).

The mention is brief; the book does not explore Hanson's wider corpus.

## Background

Robin Hanson (b. 1959) is an American economist and Associate Professor at George Mason University. He is one of the founders of the **prediction-market** field (designing the Foresight Exchange and early DARPA-related markets), and the author of *The Age of Em* (2016) and *The Elephant in the Brain* (with Kevin Simler, 2018). His 2013 article *Shall We Vote on Values, But Bet on Beliefs?* proposed [[Futarchy]] — governance by prediction markets predicting policy effects on agreed welfare measures.
