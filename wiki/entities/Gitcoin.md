## Description

Gitcoin is the largest deployment of [[Quadratic-Funding]] in the wild. From [[plurality-book]] chapter **5-7 Social Markets**, Gitcoin Grants connect sponsors (matching-fund providers, small donors and large) using a Plural funding formula that weighs the diversity of contributors more than the gross amount donated.

## Scale

At the time of the book the platform had channeled **over $100M** to public-goods projects. Funding has been especially important for:

- Web3-related projects;
- Taiwan civic-tech;
- and the production of [[plurality-book]] itself. The book includes a figure (5-7-B) showing the Plurality book Gitcoin page with $332.84 from 87 contributors at the time of writing.

## Significance

Gitcoin is held up in the book as the canonical example that **Plural funding works in practice** — not as an ideal but as a running mechanism through which millions of dollars flow. It is also explicitly cited in the [[Policy]] chapter as the kind of public-interest funding flow that [[Digital-Public-Infrastructure]] investment should aim to scale.
