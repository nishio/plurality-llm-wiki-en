## In the book

[[plurality-book]] names Ayn Rand only in passing in chapter **2-0**: "Fiction has been the central inspiration for libertarian thinking, including the work of Ayn Rand and [Neal] Stephenson." *Atlas Shrugged* (1957) is cited in a footnote as a foundational text.

The mention is part of the book's sketch of the [[Libertarianism]] worldview it positions [[Plurality]] against — not an engagement with Rand's philosophy in detail.

## Background

Ayn Rand (1905–1982) was a Russian-American novelist and philosopher, founder of the philosophical system she called Objectivism. Her best-known novels are *The Fountainhead* (1943) and *Atlas Shrugged* (1957). She is widely cited as a foundational influence on American libertarian thought, particularly in Silicon Valley.
