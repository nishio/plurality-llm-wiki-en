## Role in the manuscript

Hal Seki is the **Japanese Civic Tech leader** added in PR #1049 / #1056 ([[pluralitybook-upstream-2024-2026]]) to the GovTech / Civic Tech paragraph of chapter 3-3 [[The-Lost-Dao]]. He sits in the EN manuscript directly alongside Jennifer Pahlka's **Code for America** and Beth Simone Noveck's **The GovLab**.

## What he did (per the manuscript)

The current manuscript text (after PR #1056) reads:

> "Hal Seki, a leader in Japan's Civic Tech movement, led the creation of **sinsai.info**, a data collection and visualization platform developed after the **Great East Japan Earthquake in 2011**, and later founded [Code for Japan](https://www.code4japan.org/en)."

PR #1049's earlier wording was shorter ("Hal Seki, founder of code4japan after the Great East Japan Earthquake in 2011"); PR #1056 corrected the sequence — sinsai.info came first, Code for Japan was founded later.

## Why this matters for the wiki森

Hal Seki is another case of **JA → EN reverse integration** by a Japanese contributor (Taiyou). What the EN manuscript previously gave as a US-centric Civic Tech lineage now has a Japan-anchored second pole. The sinsai.info / 2011 earthquake framing is specifically *disaster-response* civic tech, which is a different genealogy than the US Code4America (Jennifer Pahlka) line of "open-source style government delivery" — and the manuscript text now lets readers see both.

Hal Seki appears in this EN wiki *only* because of his addition to the EN manuscript; the JA wiki almost certainly carries a much richer page on him.
