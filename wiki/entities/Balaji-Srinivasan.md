## In the book

[[plurality-book]] names Balaji Srinivasan only briefly in chapter **2-0**. *The Network State* (2022) is listed as one of the canonical texts of contemporary [[Libertarianism]], alongside *The Sovereign Individual* by James Dale Davidson and Lord William Rees-Mogg, [[Curtis-Yarvin]]'s *Unqualified Reservations*, and *Bronze Age Mindset*. [[Peter-Thiel]] is identified as the central intellectual leader connecting these authors.

The mention is used to characterize a worldview — atomistic individuals, cryptography supplanting collective politics, "exit" over "voice", floating cities and seasteads — that [[Plurality]] is offered as an alternative to.

## Background

Balaji Srinivasan (b. 1980) is an Indian-American entrepreneur and investor. He co-founded the genomics company Counsyl and the crypto firm Earn.com (acquired by Coinbase, where he then served as CTO), and was a general partner at Andreessen Horowitz. His self-published 2022 book *The Network State* argues that internet-native communities should evolve into geographically diffuse states that eventually win territorial recognition.
