## In the book

[[plurality-book]] mentions Ray Kurzweil only in passing in chapter **2-0**, grouping his "predictive futurism" with [[Nick-Bostrom]]'s and the science fiction of [[Isaac-Asimov]] and Iain M. Banks as illustrations of the [[Technocracy]] vision of AI superintelligence. Kurzweil's *The Age of Spiritual Machines* (1999) is cited in a footnote. The book lists his work alongside films like *Terminator* and *Her* as cultural touchstones for the worldview Plurality positions itself against.

## Background

Ray Kurzweil (b. 1948) is an American inventor, computer scientist and futurist. He is best known for his books *The Age of Spiritual Machines* (1999) and *The Singularity Is Near* (2005), which articulated the "technological singularity" thesis — that artificial intelligence will, on a predictable timeline, exceed human intelligence and transform civilization. He has worked at Google since 2012.
