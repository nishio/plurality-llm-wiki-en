## Role

From [[plurality-book]], E. Glen Weyl is:

- co-author of the book with [[Audrey-Tang]];
- founder of **[[RadicalxChange]]**;
- founder of Microsoft Research's **Plural Technology Collaboratory** and the **Plurality Institute**;
- co-author of *Radical Markets: Uprooting Capitalism and Democracy for a Just Society* (with Eric Posner);
- credited in the book as the modern inventor of **[[Quadratic-Voting]]** and a developer of **[[Quadratic-Funding]]**.

## Intellectual arc

The book is the most prominent statement of Weyl's evolution from the "Radical Markets" framing toward Plurality. The relationship between the two: Radical Markets focused on market-mechanism reform; Plurality embeds those reforms in a broader vision spanning identity, association, deliberation, and creative collaboration. Weyl describes himself in earlier writing as having moved from a "market design" to a "Plural" stance ("Why I am a Pluralist", RadicalxChange Blog 2022, cited in the book).

## Beyond the book

- Background in economics (Princeton PhD).
- Has worked at Microsoft Research throughout the period covered by the book.
- Public-facing essayist on technology and democracy.

## Updates

### Ongoing collaboration with Tang

From [[audreyt-org-profile]] (May 2026 snapshot of audreyt.org): Weyl and Tang co-authored *Community by Design* (arXiv:2502.10834, February 2025) with four additional co-authors. On the civic.ai site this paper is described as "the technical backbone behind Packs 1 and 5" of [[Audrey-Tang]]'s [[Civic-AI]] 6-Pack of Care — "proposing rebuilding social platforms around social fabric — rewarding content that bridges communities rather than maximising engagement."

Tang now characterises her own project as **techno-communitarianism**, "a synthesis of the Plurality framework with Glen Weyl, and Patrick Deneen's communitarian critique of liberalism — as a theory of Civic AI that fosters rather than atomises community" (from [[audreyt-org-profile]]).

### Civic AI Conference 2026

From [[democracy-needs-civic-ai]]: Weyl was a keynote speaker at the **Civic AI Conference 2026** held at Rhodes House, Oxford, on 25 March 2026 — convened by Tang and Caroline Green as the inaugural public gathering of the 6-Pack of Care community.
