## In the book

[[plurality-book]] names Andrew Yang only in passing in chapter **2-0**: "The political campaigns of Andrew Yang in the United States helped bring this perspective to the mainstream of politics" — referring to the [[Technocracy]] / [[AGI]] worldview centered on AI-driven labor displacement and [[Universal-Basic-Income]]. The book lists him alongside commentators like Ezra Klein, Matthew Yglesias and Noah Smith as evidence of the worldview's spread.

## Background

Andrew Yang (b. 1975) is an American entrepreneur and politician. He founded the non-profit Venture for America in 2011, ran in the 2020 US Democratic presidential primary on a platform built around a "Freedom Dividend" UBI of $1,000 per month per adult, and later co-founded the Forward Party. His campaign popularized UBI and AI-displacement concerns in US political discourse.
