## Description

RadicalxChange (RxC) is the non-profit, originally founded by [[Glen-Weyl]], that develops and promotes Plurality-aligned mechanisms. From [[plurality-book]], RxC has implemented Plural Voting designs — including a **Quadratic Liquid Democracy** system — for its own internal governance, treating itself as a live testbed.

## Role in the book

The book repeatedly cites RxC as a developer of:

- [[Quadratic-Voting]] and [[Quadratic-Funding]];
- correlation-discounting and eigenvoting research (Ohlhaver, Weyl and Buterin; Joel Miller, Weyl and Leon Erichsen, "Beyond Collusion Resistance" 2023);
- the **Plural Management** research that underpins the [[Plural-Management-Protocol]] used to produce the book itself.

Weyl's "Why I am a Pluralist" essay on the RadicalxChange blog (2022) is cited as a key step in the intellectual evolution from Radical Markets to Plurality.
