## In the book

[[plurality-book]] mentions Academia Sinica briefly in chapter **2-1 A View From Yushan**, describing [[Hu-Shih]] as "President of the national research institute Academia Sinica and a leading intellectual" whose fusion of Confucian tradition with Deweyan pragmatism shaped Taiwan's educational system. The mention is a single sentence; the book does not develop Academia Sinica as a topic in itself.

## Background

Academia Sinica (中央研究院, "Central Research Academy") is the national academy of Taiwan, founded in Nanjing in 1928 and relocated to Taipei in 1949 with the ROC government. It conducts research across the humanities, social sciences, mathematics and natural sciences, and oversees a network of national research institutes. Its presidents have included Hu Shih (1958–1962), Lee Yuan-tseh (1994–2006, the 1986 chemistry Nobel laureate), and the current president James C. Liao.
