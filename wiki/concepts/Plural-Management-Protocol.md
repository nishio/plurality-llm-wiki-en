## Definition

The Plural Management Protocol (often "⿻ Management Protocol" in the book's original layout) is the governance system used to produce [[plurality-book]] itself. Per chapter **5-0 Collaborative Technology and Democracy**, it combines:

- a **community currency** tracking contributor credits (a "crowd-funding" approach to recognition and reward),
- **group identity tokens** linking contributors to relevant communities (described in [[Identity-and-Personhood]] and [[Commerce-and-Trust]]),
- **advanced voting and prediction markets** for approving changes to the text ([[Plural-Voting]]),
- **[[Augmented-Deliberation]]** for resolving contentious issues,
- an open-source ledger system called **GitRules** instead of purely financial incentives,
- a **CC0** legal frame so the work is fully reusable.

The book cites the protocol's formal description: South, Erichsen, Jain, Maymounkov, Moore and Weyl, "Plural Management" (2024).

## Significance

The book uses the protocol reflexively. The argument is that the practices [[plurality-book]] *describes* are the same practices it is *produced under* — Section 5's tools are demonstrated, not merely catalogued. Contributors are recognized through the protocol; book financing was supported through [[Quadratic-Funding]] (the book includes its own Gitcoin grants page as a figure).
