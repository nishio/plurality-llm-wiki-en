## Definition

**Civic AI** is "alignment by public process: community-authored safeguards, public accountability, and bounded local systems," developed by [[Audrey-Tang]] with **Caroline Green** at the Oxford Institute for Ethics in AI as her Senior Accelerator Fellowship project after stepping down as Taiwan's Digital Minister.

From [[civic-ai-manifesto]]: it is positioned as the horizontal alternative to the vertical "singularity" trajectory — instead of one general intelligence that becomes humanity's *gardener*, Civic AI deploys many bounded local stewards (*Kami*) tending specific communities.

## The 6-Pack of Care

The framework's design primitives, derived from [[Joan-Tronto]]'s political ethics of care:

| # | Pack | Tronto phase | Core question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Attentiveness** | Caring about | What do the people closest to the pain notice that we're missing? |
| 2 | **Responsibility** | Taking care of | Who is accountable, with what authority, and what happens if they fail? |
| 3 | **Competence** | Care-giving | Does the system demonstrably work — audited, explainable, safe-to-fail? |
| 4 | **Responsiveness** | Care-receiving | Can those affected correct the system, and does correction actually change it? |
| 5 | **Solidarity** | Caring with | Does the ecosystem structurally reward cooperation over lock-in? |
| 6 | **Symbiosis** | Kami of Care | Is the system bounded, sunset-ready, and incapable of imperial creep? |

Packs 1–4 form Tronto's feedback loop. Pack 5 (from *Caring Democracy*) scales the loop across organisations. Pack 6 is Tang and Green's addition — the meta-level guardrail.

## The Kami

The **unit of deployment is the Kami** — bounded local steward inspired by Japanese Shinto guardian spirits. From [[civic-ai-manifesto]]: "There might be a local Kami of a specific river, a particular forest, or even an old tree. Whatever the form, its entire existence and purpose are interwoven with the health of that one thing."

Key design principles:

- **Boundedness**: resource caps, sunset timers, non-expansion pacts, fresh democratic authority required for scope change.
- **Federation + Subsidiarity**: Kamis interact under shared rules; problems are solved at the most local level possible.
- "A society of AI permaculturists" rather than a singleton.

## Relationship to [[Plurality]]

From [[civic-ai-manifesto]]: "The 6-Pack is an application of ⿻ Plurality to AI governance." Tang frames Civic AI as the AI-governance face of the broader Plurality programme:
- horizontal (vs. vertical) intelligence
- bridge-making (Polis, Community Notes) as the social-media-stack instantiation
- Alignment Assemblies (Taiwan's 447-citizen 2024 assembly on deepfake scams) as the policy instantiation

## Concrete instantiations

From [[collaborative-immune-system]] and [[ai-alignment-cannot-be-top-down]]: Taiwan's 2024 Alignment Assembly → *Fraud Crime Hazard Prevention Act* → 95%+ drop in impersonation scam ads by 2025.

From [[good-enough-ancestor-senate-canada]]: Polis bridging algorithm adopted on X.com as Community Notes; Collaborative Notes drafted by AI and instantly corrected by humans teaches AI to translate across communities.

From [[safer-sovereignty]]: enterprise Civic AI = bounded local stewards under federated safety arrangements, replacing the "corporate singleton" mainframe-mentality.
