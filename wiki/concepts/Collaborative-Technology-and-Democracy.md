## Definition

"Collaborative Technology and Democracy" is the title of chapter **5-0** of [[plurality-book]], and the section's framing concept. The chapter argues that democracy, properly conceived, is far broader than national voting — it is *any* technology that lets several entities (people or groups) cooperate to produce something greater than the sum of their separate efforts (mathematically: a **supermodular** outcome).

## The depth-breadth spectrum

The chapter sets out a "production possibilities frontier" between **depth** of collaboration (how much extra value is created per pair of collaborators) and **breadth** (number and diversity of participants). Existing modes occupy points on this frontier:

- **Commodity** end (broadest, thinnest): capitalist exchange via money.
- **Community** end (deepest, narrowest): intimacy, family, deep trust.
- **State** in between: bureaucracy, voting, conversation.

Plural technologies aim to push the frontier outward at every point. The book illustrates this with a chart placing each of Section 5's chapters on the frontier:

- **[[Post-Symbolic-Communication]]** — deepest, narrowest.
- **[[Immersive-Shared-Reality]]** — slightly broader, very deep.
- **[[Creative-Collaborations]]** — deep and broader.
- **[[Augmented-Deliberation]]** — middle.
- **[[Adaptive-Administration]]** — broader, less deep.
- **[[Plural-Voting]]** — thinner, broader.
- **[[Social-Markets]]** — broadest, thinnest.

## The book as living proof of concept

Chapter 5-0 also reflexively notes that [[plurality-book]] itself was produced using these technologies: Git, [[Quadratic-Funding]] (via Gitcoin), advanced voting and prediction markets for change approval, community currencies, and the [[Plural-Management-Protocol]] — all governed under a CC0 license.

## Three regenerating concerns

The chapter develops three cross-cutting concerns:

1. **Collaboration across diversity is energy-like** — like fossil fuels, diversity can either combust dangerously (conflict) or be harnessed productively. Plural technology builds the engines.
2. **Premature optimization is dangerous** — committing to a single welfare function ahead of social learning leads to oppressive systems (Tony Hoare: "the root of all evil").
3. **Diversity must be regenerated**, not just harnessed. Bridges that consume diversity without replenishing it deplete the resource.
