## Summary
The README is the canonical hub indexing Tang's post-ministerial work. Beyond the *Plurality* book, it lists her current roles (Taiwan Cyber Ambassador, Senior Accelerator Fellow at Oxford's Institute for Ethics in AI, TED 2026 Guest Curator, Mozilla Foundation Omidyar Senior Advisor, Project Liberty Institute Senior Fellow, Ethereum Foundation Plurality Initiative Advisor), recent recognitions (2025 Right Livelihood Award; 2025 AI World Society World Leader Award at Harvard's Loeb House; 2023 TIME 100 AI), and recent peer-reviewed publications.

## Key claims (about Tang's current work)

- Tang is "developing **techno-communitarianism** — a synthesis of the Plurality framework with [[Glen-Weyl]], and Patrick Deneen's communitarian critique of liberalism — as a theory of Civic AI that fosters rather than atomises community."
- 2025 Right Livelihood Award citation: "advancing the social use of digital technology to empower citizens, renew democracy and heal divides."
- Right Livelihood acceptance speech quote: "We built a geothermal democracy — the crashing plates turned into positive energy for renewal." (Stockholm, December 2025)
- Tang served as Taiwan's first digital minister; under her tenure public trust in government rose "from single digits to over 70 percent" — innovations cited: the real-time Mask Map and 1922 SMS during COVID-19, prebunking defences against cyber interference, [[Join]].gov.tw.
- "I want to be a good enough ancestor for future generations."
- "I insist on relinquishing copyright because if I disappear tomorrow, future generations can make use of the materials that I have." (from the *Good Enough Ancestor* documentary by Cynthia Wade)
- Cyber Ambassador role since 2024 (ambassadorial); "the platforms, the methodology, and the legislation outlasted the tenure, which is what 'complete the work and step back' was designed to mean in practice."
