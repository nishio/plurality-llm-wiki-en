## Summary
Tang argues that AI is not mere automation — it "redistributes attention, authority, and bargaining power" — and that in a democracy such redistribution must be visible, contestable, and co-governed. The testimony introduces three rights to add to the work-rights tradition (right to learn, right to know, right to contest), the six governance questions of the Civic AI 6-Pack, and the slogans "data as soil, not oil", "campfires not wildfires", and "sovereignty is not solitude".

## Key claims

- AI in the workplace must guarantee three rights: **right to learn** (training *before* displacement), **right to know** (transparency on whether AI is being used and whose data shapes the outcome), **right to contest** (appeals that lead to repair without requiring a CS degree).
- "A black-box outcome should not be treated as due process."
- Inclusive prosperity is democratic security. To the agenda of *protecting*, *empowering*, and *building*, add a fourth verb: **co-governing**.
- A democratic AI system must be **interruptible** — possible to pause, override, or retire without disrupting essential services.
- Alignment Assembly recap (200,000 SMS → 447 citizens → 85% consensus → 90%+ reduction in impersonation/deepfake ads through 2025). "When people want to show up at the table, the idea is not to do top-down control. It is to invent a bigger table."
- **Data as soil, not oil**: domain-specific local models are energy-efficient because they don't have to anticipate every use; communities cultivate their data as soil. Taiwan's TCloud subsidises *the freedom to choose* up to 80% of SaaS purchase, going to SMEs not vendors. Result: interoperability, data sovereignty, sector-trained models, drone-agriculture co-ops achieving previously-only-big-agribusiness scale.
- **Zero-knowledge contact tracing during COVID**: venue-printed QR random number → SMS to 1922 → telecom learns nothing, venue learns nothing (not even visitor's phone number), state learns nothing — but infection contact tracing still works.
- **Campfires, not wildfires**: Authoritarian intelligence (personalised feeds, engagement-through-enragement) vs. **assistive intelligence** (Polis-style cross-conversation between those who would otherwise not agree). "Polis makes overlap viral. Only bridge-makers gain virality." X.com Community Notes is the adopted form; new Collaborative Notes drafted by AI and instantly corrected by humans teaches AI to translate across communities (e.g. climate-justice ↔ biblical creation care).
- **Interoperable governance** beats one-size-fits-all global rules: auditing standards, incident-reporting, synthetic-media provenance, anti-lock-in procurement — works across jurisdictions without harmonising every domestic rule. "Canada, Taiwan, and other free and open societies can be peers in this work."
- **Sovereign AI** in Taiwan = "a reproducible process for language communities to *own* the social and cultural composition of the data" — not just a Mandarin-Taigi-Hakka-Indigenous model. Taiwan has 16 Indigenous nations and 42+ language variations; *transcultural* sovereignty (translation capability) is paired with cultural sovereignty.
- "Sovereignty is not solitude. It is the way to protect people and cooperate without surrendering public judgment."
- Epigraph: "A good enough ancestor does not ask 'Are the machines ready to replace humans?' A good enough ancestor asks how machines can help humans better care for one another, deliberate together and keep faith with those not yet born."
