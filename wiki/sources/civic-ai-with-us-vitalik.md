## Summary

Buterin endorses the [[Civic-AI]] framing of AI as a bounded local Kami rather than a vertical singleton, situating his own concerns about *bounding authority and delegation* and *privacy-preserving shadow LLMs* inside the 6-Pack of Care vocabulary. He warns that the dominant AI vision is "homogenising" and proposes per-user and per-relationship fine-tuning as the antidote.

## Key claims

- **Critique of mainstream AI vision**: "cubic-kilometre data centres that are copies of the same thing and probably running the same thing" — UBI for the unemployed is presented as benevolent but assumes a benevolent political system; humans psychologically need *agency*, not just consumption.
- **AI as a bicycle for the mind**: "not a car that you're supposed to just go to sleep inside." AI should maximise human-AI collaboration, be pluralistic, and be customised to existing things-people-are-doing.
- **Bounding authority and delegation**: "If you just give one of [the agents] your private key, even if you tell it to pay for your coffee, it might get tricked by something on the internet." Money is the obvious case for bounded delegation; *privacy* is the next.
- **Shadow LLMs as civic tech**: "a sort of LLM shadow of yourself that then gets put into a box and it gets to see all kinds of secret information from other people, from the organisation. And it's able to output a decision — just that one decision — based on what that secret information is, and it can't leak anything else." Solves the distributed-decision-making problem of "too many decision-makers to share secrets with." MPC/FHE/ZK as enabling tech.
- **Community translation**: AI as bridge between communities with different languages/styles — "take that [a paper from another community] and put it into a bot and have the bot explain it to me in the style that I understand." A scoped, near-term application.
- **Resisting AI monoculture**: "A few models that everyone speaks through" — even open-source models distil from closed ones; "flattening the world in a big way." Counter-vision: "much more hybrid AI-human works that actually feel like an expression of a lot of different people's different ideas."
- **Per-relationship fine-tuning**: "Ideally, every person should have a model fine-tuned to them. Ideally, every pair of people that talks to each other should have a model fine-tuned to their relationship." (Quoted back at length by Tenzin Yangtso in [[civic-ai-gentle-bridge]].)

## Significance for the wiki

This is the canonical 2026 statement of *Buterin endorsing Tang's Civic-AI framing*. Prior to this, Buterin and Tang had been understood as adjacent-but-distinct (Buterin → d/acc + AI-as-player-in-prediction-markets; Tang → bridging + bounded Kami). This video makes their position substantially convergent on the *near-term implementation question*: bounded local AI tuned to specific relationships, with cryptographic privacy guarantees, deployed as civic infrastructure.

The "shadow LLM" idea is the most technically novel piece — it offers a concrete answer to the "data coalition cannot deliberate because secrets cannot be shared" objection that has dogged the book's Section 4-4 (MIDs / data fiduciaries).
