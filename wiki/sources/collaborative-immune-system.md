## Summary
Tang explains how Taiwan converted an Alignment Assembly's 85%+ consensus on AI scam-ad regulation into the *Fraud Crime Hazard Prevention Act* and its Chapter 3 governing Internet Advertising Platforms. The address details the four "lines of defence" (KYA verification, transparency labelling, 24-hour notice-and-takedown with joint liability, and graduated enforcement up to DNS blocking) and reports a 95%+ drop in impersonation scam ads.

## Key claims

- **85% consensus**: 447-citizen Alignment Assembly in March 2024 produced (a) 89% support for AI-content detection/labelling, (b) 85% support for platform joint liability, (c) consensus on traffic throttling for foreign non-compliers.
- "In a democracy, the strongest anti-fraud technology is not a filter. It is legitimacy." Because the public wrote the rules themselves, there was no backlash when the law passed.
- Four lines of legal defence: **KYA** (Know Your Advertiser, Article 30) — digital signatures or biometrics required before any ad runs; **Radical Transparency** (Article 31) — advertiser/funder disclosure and explicit deepfake labelling; **Notice & Takedown** (Article 32) — 24-hour removal window with joint civil liability for non-removal, plus safe harbour for good-faith removal (Article 38); **Enforcement** (Article 39) — graduated penalties: NT$100M fines, traffic throttling, DNS blocking.
- "This applies only to advertising — paid pushed content. This is about *Reach*, not Freedom of Speech."
- Results: impersonation scam ads ↓ 95%+ (from >30,000/week to <1,000/week); investment scam ads ↓ ~90%; total accepted fraud cases ↓ 25% Nov-2024→Nov-2025; reported financial losses ↓ 50%+. 112,054 suspected scam ads removed from Meta August 2024 → November 2025.
- AI must fight AI ("以AI治AI"). Tang serves on the board of **ROOST** (Robust Open Online Safety Tools), which provides the `gpt-oss-safeguard` model — deliberative (cites the rule it acted on), open (locally fine-tunable), steerable (rules are a text file, no GPU retrain needed).
- Closing: "Where there is a will, there is a way." Taiwan's agile social implementation + Japan's deep rule-of-law tradition = "Free the Future, together."
