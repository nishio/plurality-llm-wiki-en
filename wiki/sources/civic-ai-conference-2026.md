## Summary

This is the structural source for the entire 6-Pack-of-Care conference: not a transcript itself, but the agenda + opening framing. It is valuable because it names every speaker who appeared at Oxford and provides the most authoritative answer to "who is in the Civic-AI orbit beyond Tang and Green?"

## Stated goals
1. **Shared understanding** — everyone on the same page about Civic AI.
2. **Safe space** — difficult questions raised openly.
3. **Real impact** — outcomes feed into the *Civic AI* book project.

## Opening quotations

- **HH Dalai Lama XIV**: "In the end, AI is a tool for this world. … We must use these tools not for the sake of control, but to improve the pathways of human connection."
- **Pope Leo XIV** (citing *Magnifica Humanitas*, May 2026): "True progress always stems from a heart open to others, an intelligence willing to listen and a will that seeks what unites rather than what separates."

## Significance for the wiki

Until this source was ingested, the EN wiki had pages only for Tang, Green, and Tronto in the Civic-AI orbit. This source establishes that the Civic-AI movement at Oxford has a broader bench of voices including the *Tibetan-Buddhist ethics* track (Geshe Lobsang Negi, Tenzin Gayche, Tenzin Yangtso), the *affective-computing* track (Picard), the *disability-rights / lived-experience* track (Farquharson), the *gender-and-care* track (Slaughter, on her *Daedalus* 2025 paper with Elizabeth Garlow on *sapiens integra*), and the *Embassy Network / network of houses* track (Agnew).

The fact that Pope Leo XIV is cited from a May 2026 encyclical (*Magnifica Humanitas*) is itself a data point: the Civic-AI / 6-Pack-of-Care framing has reached the Vatican-level moral-authority discourse by mid-2026.
