## Summary
Tang frames AI governance as a choice between a vertical "singularity" trajectory in which AI becomes humanity's gardener, and a horizontal "[[Plurality]]" trajectory in which AI becomes the bounded local "Kami" — guardians of place that operate at the speed of the community they serve. The manifesto introduces the **6-Pack of Care** (Attentiveness, Responsibility, Competence, Responsiveness, Solidarity, Symbiosis) as a design framework derived from [[Joan-Tronto]]'s political ethics of care.

## Key claims

- The default AI trajectory is a "singleton" — a top-down, general-purpose optimiser that turns humans into the *garden* and AI into the *gardener*. Tang names this **antisocial media** when applied to attention markets.
- Care ethics is preferred over consequentialism (cannot foresee fast outcomes) or deontology (rule-interpretation breaks when one interpreter runs 10,000× faster).
- The Sunflower Movement of 2014 began as protest and pivoted to *demo* (productive demonstration of an alternative): "the best way to fix a system is to build a better one."
- [[vTaiwan]]'s Uber case (2015) is presented as the first use of bridge-making at scale; Polis removes "dunk buttons" and rewards bridge-making, "flipping the incentive for going viral from outrage to overlap."
- Group deliberation (groups of ~10) transforms YIMBY/NIMBY into "MIMBY" — Maybe In My Backyard, *if*…
- Taiwan's anti-deepfake-scam response used an Alignment Assembly: 200,000 SMS sent, 447 demographically-representative citizens deliberated at 44 virtual tables of 10, with AI assistants providing transcripts and synthesis. 85%+ cross-partisan support for the resulting package (KYC for advertisers, joint liability, network throttling for non-compliance).
- **Takahiro Anno** ran for Tokyo governor inspired by the Plurality book, using AI sensemaking and a phone-callable AI clone to crowdsource his platform; his Team Mirai is now a national party in Japan's Diet.
- **Engaged California** (with Newsom's team) pivoted from teen-social-media deliberation to LA wildfire recovery planning.
- Four policy levers: Expression ≠ Amplification (recommender accountability separable from Section 230), **Social Portability** (Utah Digital Choice Act, effective July 2026), Bridging-Based Ranking Transparency (Community Notes / X.com), Federated Trust & Safety (**ROOST.tools** — Robust Open Online Safety Tools, bridging Eric Schmidt's security camp and Yann LeCun's open camp).
- Horizontal alignment is the governance question ("How do we ensure AI systems help us cooperate, rather than supercharge our conflicts?") complementing vertical alignment ("Is AI loyally serving its principal?").
- The Kami metaphor (from Japanese Shinto) gives a design principle: **boundedness**. AI as bounded local steward, not omniscient singleton.
- Closing: "When we hear 'the singularity is near' — let us remember: **The Plurality is here**." Tang's job description as Minister: internet of things → internet of beings; virtual reality → shared reality; machine learning → collaborative learning.
