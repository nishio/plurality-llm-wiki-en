## Summary

Slaughter reads the 6-Pack of Care through her own decade-long work on care, gender equity, and *sapiens integra* (whole-human) — a concept she developed first with Hilary Cottam and then with Elizabeth Garlow in a January 2025 *Daedalus* article ("A Worldview of Care and a New Economics"). She finds the [[Civic-AI]] Kami framework "revelatory" because it extends Tronto's ethics of care "to a civic system that must be relational but is not necessarily human."

## Key claims

- **Maslow inverted**: the standard pyramid (physical needs → love/belonging → self-actualisation at the top, individualistic) is wrong. Self-actualisation and love/belonging should be *parallel needs of equal strength*. Connection and separation are simultaneous from birth.
- ***Sapiens integra***: a name for the whole human, who needs "connection as much as separation, belonging as much as self-actualisation." First coined with Hilary Cottam, developed in the Slaughter–Garlow *Daedalus* paper.
- **A worldview of care, first proposition**: "strong connections are essential to human health and well-being; disconnection and misconnection can be fatal." Relational perspective grounding the rest.
- **The Kami is "a spirit that is born of relationship"** — not external animism. "My relationship to the beauty of my garden, or to the place I live, or within my family."
- **Ethics vs. ethos**: "Ethics are the principles, but the ethos is the spirit, the character, that which we are grounded in and informs us." The 6-Pack is the ethics; care is the ethos.
- **Horizontal not vertical**: aligns with Tang's framing — "the web rather than the ladder" (Carol Gilligan). "The gardener and the garden are in reciprocal relationship" — neither AI gardens humans nor humans garden AI; they shape one another.
- **The convergence**: "between care, which is deeply, deeply human, and AI, which is machine, and yet through these principles and through this ethos, we can find a bridge that holds enormous potential."

## Significance for the wiki

This is the *gender-and-care* lineage's voice in the Civic AI conversation — distinct from the digital-democracy and crypto-side lineages. Slaughter's *sapiens integra* concept gives Civic AI a concept of the *kind of human* it presupposes — analogous to but distinct from Plurality's "humans intersecting in plural publics." Where Plurality describes the *social* structure, *sapiens integra* describes the *psychological* condition the social structure is supposed to support.
